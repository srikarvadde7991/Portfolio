How I think about it

Vulnerability management approach

A practical, layered flow — from discovery through remediation — built around risk-based prioritization rather than raw scan counts.

1 · Discovery & Scanning

  • Qualys VMDR & Tenable.SC / Nessus
  • Authenticated scanning across Windows & Linux
  • Web app scanning (Qualys WAS, Tenable)
  • Asset & IP subnet coverage management

2 · Analysis & Prioritization

  • CVSS scoring + EPSS exploit probability
  • CISA KEV correlation for active exploitation
  • Asset criticality & business context
  • False-positive validation

3 · Remediation & Tracking

  • SLA-based remediation workflows
  • ServiceNow (CMDB) ticketing & ownership
  • Revalidation scans to confirm closure
  • Aging & backlog reduction

4 · Reporting & Dashboards

  • Power BI / Tableau operational dashboards
  • MTTR, aging, and SLA KPIs
  • Exploitable vs. non-exploitable views
  • Executive & stakeholder summaries
